         5. DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSION

The contribution is measured concentration across paired
comparisons on this fixed benchmark, not a new confidence
procedure. A diffuse delete-one profile across the affected
SSL pairs would contradict the concentration claim; Table 2
exposes both the strong flagship case and the weaker third
pair. A changed speaker-only decision would contradict the
reported primary factor agreement. Neither finding estab-
lishes why those groups matter, and SpoofCeleb shows why
the factor pattern should not become a universal prescription.
     For a small paired EER advantage on this benchmark,
report the point difference, trial and group bands for the same
family, and influential-group deletion sensitivity. If only the
trial band excludes zero, describe the advantage as sensitive
to evaluation composition and seek broader group support
before claiming robust superiority. Publish the group mem-
berships and weighting rules needed to reproduce the check.
