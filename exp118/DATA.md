# EXP-118 data

Inputs are the EXP-114 manifest of record and the four original EXP-114 score tables,
extracted from `~/data/corpora/anti-spoofing/spoofceleb/derived/EXP-114-m1-spoofceleb-confirmation/`
(`EXP-114-run-artifacts.tar` for `scores/*.tsv`, `EXP-114-provenance-rerun-20260830.tar` for
`inputs/spoofceleb_evaluation.csv`). They are license-gated SpoofCeleb derivatives and are
not committed; `inputs` is a local symlink to the extraction directory. `RESULTS.json`
records their SHA-256.
